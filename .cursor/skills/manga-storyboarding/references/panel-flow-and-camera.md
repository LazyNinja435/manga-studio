# Panel Flow, Camera Grammar & Action Choreography Reference

## Shot Scale Abbreviations

| Code | Shot Type | Typical Narrative Use |
|---|---|---|
| `ELS` | Extreme Long Shot | World scale, isolation, army/landscape establishing |
| `LS` / `WS` | Long / Wide Shot | Full-body choreography, spatial relationship between fighters |
| `MS` | Medium Shot | Waist-up gesture, body language + dialogue interaction |
| `MCU` | Medium Close-Up | Chest/shoulders-up intimate conversation, subtle tension |
| `CU` | Close-Up | Facial expression, emotional shift, critical prop/hand detail |
| `ECU` | Extreme Close-Up | Eyes widening, blade edge, trembling fingers, micro-impact |

## Eye-Flow & Vector Rules

1. **RTL Eye Entry & Exit**:
   - Reader enters panel near **upper-right** (usually first speech balloon), drops through focal character/action, and exits toward **lower-left** or the next panel's entry corner.
2. **Directional Motion Vectors**:
   - **In RTL**: Motion traveling `right -> left` accelerates with natural eye movement; motion traveling `left -> right` pushes against the eye, creating friction, shock, or defensive walling.
   - **In LTR**: Reverse horizontal polarity (`left -> right` accelerates; `right -> left` resists).
3. **4-Beat Combat Exchange Chain**:
   - **Beat 1 (Wind-up / Intent)**: Stance shift, gaze lock, or weapon draw.
   - **Beat 2 (Trajectory / Closing)**: Speedlines, foreshortened rush, or projectile arc.
   - **Beat 3 (Impact / Clash)**: High-contrast contact frame (wide or splash), clear contact point, strong black/white flash.
   - **Beat 4 (Aftermath / Reaction)**: Skid, recoil, damage reveal, or silent reaction beat.

## Gutter & Border Vocabulary

- **Standard white gutter (4–6 mm vertical, 6–9 mm horizontal tiers)**: Normal narrative time flow; wider horizontal tier gutter signals slightly longer beat between rows than between side-by-side panels.
- **Black page gutters**: Flashbacks, nightmares, subconscious realms, or oppressive dread.
- **Borderless / Open panel**: Timeless emotion, memory drift, or quiet atmospheric expansion.
- **Full-bleed panel / Splash**: Peak dramatic release extending to page trim + 3 mm bleed.
