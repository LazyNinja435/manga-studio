# Dual-Edition Artistic Treatment Matrix

How identical dramatic elements translate natively between **Full-Color** and **Traditional Monochrome** manga editions:

| Visual Element | Full-Color Edition Execution | Traditional Monochrome Edition Execution |
|---|---|---|
| **Foreground vs. Background Separation** | Warm/cool temperature contrast, atmospheric haze, selective saturation on focal character | Thicker foreground contour ink, white halo (*nuki*) around overlapping silhouettes, lighter line weight in depth |
| **Dark Hair / Dark Garments** | Deep hue base (indigo, espresso, charcoal) with colored rim light and specular sheen | Solid pure black ink (*beta*) broken by sharp white feather/sheen highlights (*tsuya beta*) |
| **Skin & Subtle Form Shadow** | Soft warm/cool shadow shapes with subsurface edge warmth | Pure white paper for lit skin; crisp minimal cast shadow edge under chin/brow or light 10% tone only when moody |
| **Dramatic Impact / Explosion / Clash** | High-luminance color flare, complementary shock rim lighting, flying embers/debris | Stark black-and-white flash (*beta-flash*), radiating tapered speedlines (*shūchūsen*), zero muddy gray |
| **Night / Torchlit Interior** | Deep blue-violet ambient shadow with localized warm orange key light falloff | Heavy solid black shadow blocks, directional crosshatching, 40–60% tone sky/walls, crisp white rim highlights |
| **Flashback / Memory Sequence** | Muted sepia/desaturated palette or soft vignette + black page gutters | Black page gutters + distinctive fine vertical tone or soft borderless fade |
| **Blood / Battle Damage** | Controlled dark crimson (respecting audience rating) contrasting muted dust/stone | Solid black splatter/streaks against white skin/cloth, or stark white splatter against solid black background |
