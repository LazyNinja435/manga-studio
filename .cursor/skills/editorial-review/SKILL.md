# Editorial Review

## Purpose

Deliver constructive, craft-focused manga editorial critique (*tantō henshūsha* perspective) that elevates reader engagement, emotional payoff, visual clarity, and page-turn momentum before work advances.

## Inputs

- Deliverable(s) under review (`manga/series-bible.md`, `manga/arcs/`, `manga/characters/`, `manga/chapters/ch-XX/`, assembled pages).
- `manga/manga-manifest.yaml` (`genre`, `target_audience`, `reading_direction`, `edition_mode`).

## Procedure

1. **Assess Dramatic Hook & Reader Investment**:
   - Does the opening establish an immediate emotional question, tension, or visual intrigue within the first 3 pages (or opening premise)?
   - Are character stakes personal and active rather than passive exposition?
2. **Evaluate Pacing, Rhythm & *Ma* (間)**:
   - Check compression vs. decompression: Are climactic emotional turns given room to breathe (silent beat + splash/anchor panel), while transitional logistics move briskly?
   - Verify chapter-ending hooks compel turning to the next chapter.
3. **Critique Show-Don't-Tell & Dialogue Sharpness**:
   - Flag redundant lines where characters state feelings or actions already unmistakable in the panel art.
   - Check subtext and voice differentiation: if speaker labels were hidden, would each character still sound distinct?
4. **Evaluate Visual Readability & Action Comprehension**:
   - Check for "talking-head syndrome" (monotonous chest-up shots), unclear spatial jumps in combat, cluttered silhouettes, or buried focal points.
   - Verify reveals and emotional shocks land right after a page turn in `reading_direction`.
5. **Prioritize Actionable Recommendations**:
   - Group notes into `MUST-FIX` (hurts comprehension, stakes, or readability), `SUGGESTION` (materially strengthens emotional/visual punch), and `POLISH` (optional craft refinement), naming the owning specialist for each item.

## Output

- Structured Editorial Review Report returned to the Director (recorded in `manga/production/reviews/`).

## Validation

- [ ] Review highlights concrete strengths worth protecting alongside weaknesses.
- [ ] Every critique points to specific pages/panels/sections and offers a practical craft remedy rather than vague dissatisfaction.
- [ ] Critique respects the project's declared genre, tone, and canon policy without rewriting files directly.

## Failure Handling

- **Early rough draft vs. pre-publication polish**: Calibrate critique depth to stage—focus on structural stakes and character motivation on early arc/script drafts; focus on shot rhythm, line economy, and balloon flow on storyboard/page passes.
- **Subjective creative trade-off**: Present two clear craft options with pros/cons for the user and Director rather than mandating a single stylistic preference.
